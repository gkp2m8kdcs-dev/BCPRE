About BCPRE

BCPRE is a personal investment journal for self-directed investors. It helps you manage multiple portfolios, log every buy and sell with full execution detail, attach photos of paper trade tickets, and analyze your performance over time - all stored locally on your device.

Key Features

Portfolios

Organize holdings across multiple accounts and strategies.

Trade Journal

Record every buy, sell, dividend, and split with full execution detail.

Attachments

Attach photos of paper trade tickets and notes to any trade.

Watchlist

Monitor symbols you are tracking without cluttering your portfolios.

Insights

News and economic calendar to keep your journal context-rich.

Multi-Currency

Track values in your preferred base currency.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does BCPRE connect to my broker?

No. BCPRE does not connect to any broker, exchange, or trading platform. It is a manual journaling tool. You record your own trades and the app organizes and analyzes the data.

Does BCPRE place or transmit orders?

No. The app cannot place, route, or transmit orders of any kind. All entries are stored only on your device.

Where is my data stored?

All portfolios, trades, watchlist, alerts, attachments, and preferences live exclusively on your device in the app's private storage. Nothing is uploaded to a remote server.

Does BCPRE provide investment advice?

No. BCPRE does not provide investment, financial, legal, or tax advice. Charts, quotes, and news shown in the app are illustrative for personal journaling and may be delayed or simulated.

How do I export my data?

Open Settings → Data → Export Workspace. A JSON snapshot of your portfolios, trades, watchlist, and alerts will be generated that you can save or share.

How do I reset the app?

Open Settings → Data → Reset All Data. This permanently removes all portfolios, trades, watchlist, alerts, and attachments on your device. This action cannot be undone.

How do I delete my account or data?

BCPRE does not require an account. To remove all data from your device, use the in-app Reset All Data option, or delete the app from your device.
