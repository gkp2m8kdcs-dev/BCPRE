Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how BCPRE handles your information. The short version: BCPRE does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

BCPRE is designed to function without collecting any information about you. Specifically, the app:

  • Does not require you to create an account.
  • Does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any contact information.
  • Does not collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, or analytics IDs.
  • Does not track your activity across other apps or websites.
  • Does not use cookies, pixels, or similar tracking technologies.
  • Does not transmit your portfolio, trades, watchlist, alerts, attachments, or preferences to any server.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The following information is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Portfolios, positions, and holdings you create.
  • Trade records (buys, sells, dividends, splits) you enter.
  • Watchlist symbols you add.
  • Price alerts you configure.
  • Photos and notes you attach to trades (stored in the app's private documents directory).
  • App preferences such as your selected base currency.

This data is never uploaded, synced, or shared with us or any third party. It remains under your control and can be removed at any time through the in-app Reset All Data option, or by deleting the app.

3. Information Shown by the App

BCPRE may display illustrative market data, charts, and news articles to help you contextualize the trades you journal. This content is fetched by the app and shown to you locally; it is not derived from your personal data and is not used to build a profile about you.

Charts, quotes, and news shown in the app may be delayed, sampled, or simulated. They are provided for personal journaling and educational context only and do not constitute investment advice.

4. Children

BCPRE is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). The app does not knowingly collect information from children.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Because BCPRE does not collect your data on a server, there is nothing for us to retrieve, change, or delete on your behalf. You have full control over the data on your device:

  • Access & export: Use Settings → Export Workspace to generate a JSON copy of your data.
  • Delete: Use Settings → Reset All Data, or delete the app from your device.
  • Attachments: Deleting a trade also removes its attached files from the app's private directory.

6. Third-Party Services

BCPRE does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or social SDKs. The app may fetch publicly available news content for display, but this is done without identifying you.

7. Security

Because your data never leaves your device, its security depends primarily on the security of your device. We recommend that you:

  • Use a device passcode and biometric authentication.
  • Keep your iOS version up to date.
  • Enable Find My iPhone so you can remotely erase your device if it is lost or stolen.

Data stored by BCPRE is sandboxed by iOS and is not accessible to other apps on your device.

8. International Use

Because no personal data is transmitted from your device to us, data protection and cross-border transfer regulations (such as the GDPR or CCPA) have limited direct applicability to BCPRE. If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California, you retain the right to request information about any data we may hold about you - which, by design, is none.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the app or in applicable law. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are made. Material changes will be highlighted in the app's release notes.
